Clarifying your coverage

Your appointment may not be entirely cost-free. Before your first visit, we recommend calling the customer service number on your insurance card and asking:

  • Do I have mental health coverage under my current plan?
  • Is iTrust Wellness an in-network provider with my plan?
  • What can I expect to pay for outpatient mental health visits?
  • Does my plan cover telehealth appointments?
  • Are there limits on the number of visits covered per year?
  • Do I need a referral or prior authorization?
  • Can you confirm coverage for iTrust's CPT codes?

If your appointment isn't covered by insurance, you can opt into our self-pay model (rates above) instead.

Insurance terminology

Co-Pay

A fixed amount you pay for a covered service, usually at the time of your visit.

In-Network

Providers who have a contract with your insurance plan, which usually means lower costs for you.

Out of Network

Providers without a contract with your plan; visits may cost more or may not be covered.

Deductible

The amount you pay out of pocket each year before your insurance begins to share costs.

Explanation of Benefits (EOB)

A statement from your insurer showing what was billed, what was covered, and what you may owe. It is not a bill.

Coordination of Benefits (COB)

The process insurers use to decide who pays first when you're covered by more than one plan.

Pre-Certification

Approval from your insurer confirming a service is medically necessary before you receive it.

Premium

The amount you (or your employer) pay for your insurance coverage, usually each month.

Self-Pay

Paying for care directly without using insurance, at our published self-pay rates.

Superbill

An itemized receipt you can submit to your insurer for possible out-of-network reimbursement.
