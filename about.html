Who we are

A more personal approach to mental health

We believe care should go beyond a one-size-fits-all approach. We get to know your psyche, environment, and lifestyle, then stay close, so your treatment keeps its rhythm and you keep moving forward.

Our mission

To provide compassionate mental health services that understand the whole person, building tailored treatment plans and lasting relationships through frequent, caring touchpoints rather than disrupted treatment.

  • 6,500+ clients served across South Carolina
  • Most clients report symptom relief within 12 weeks
  • Telehealth and in-person care at 3 locations
  • LGBTQIA+ affirming, judgment-free care

What makes us different

Mental health is our priority, and so are you. Here's how that shows up in your care:

  • Individualized, pragmatic treatment plans
  • Frequent interaction and touchpoints for sustained success
  • Understanding your circumstances over standardized protocols
  • A care team that listens and keeps you informed
