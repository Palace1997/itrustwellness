How we treat

Evidence-based methods, tailored to you and adjusted as you progress.

Medication management

Thoughtful psychiatric medication management with regular check-ins to keep your plan working for you.

Genetic testing

Genetic testing helps guide medication choices, so we can find what fits your body faster.

Long-Acting Injectables (LAIs)

Convenient long-acting injectable options that simplify treatment and support consistency.

Diagnostic assessments

Thorough diagnostic assessments to understand what's really going on and chart the right path.

Substance use disorder care

Supportive, non-judgmental treatment for substance use disorders, centered on your recovery.

Personalized plans

Every plan is individualized and pragmatic — built around your psyche, environment, and lifestyle.
