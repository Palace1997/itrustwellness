Frequently asked questions

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. All three locations offer both telehealth and in-person appointments, so you can be seen from wherever you feel safe.

How quickly will I feel better?

Every journey is different, but most of our clients report symptom relief within their first 12 weeks of care.

Is your care LGBTQIA+ affirming?

Absolutely. We provide affirming, judgment-free care for everyone who walks through our doors.

How do I book?

Call 864-520-2020, book online, or start with a free assessment and we'll guide you from there.
